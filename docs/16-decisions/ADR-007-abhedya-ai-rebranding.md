# ADR-007: Official Product Rebranding from OnPremisAI to ABHEDYA AI

- **Status:** **APPROVED & ADOPTED**
- **Date:** 2026-09-09
- **Author:** Quantum Compilers Architecture Team

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement
The initial project name "OnPremisAI" sounded generic and lacked strong alignment with Indian national defense sovereignty, critical infrastructure protection, and the Smart India Hackathon theme. The visual identity (a 3D wireframe cube) was difficult to reproduce and lacked defensive symbolism.

---

## 2. Decision
Rebrand the product to **ABHEDYA AI** across all user interfaces, codebases, documentation, and presentation materials:
- **Meaning:** "Abhedya" (Sanskrit for *invulnerable*, *impenetrable*, and *unbreakable*).
- **Core Promise:** *"Intelligence that stays inside your walls."*
- **Visual Identity:** Solid pitch-black background with a defensive sovereignty shield featuring segmented Gold, Green, and Red indicators.
- **Team Identity:** Retain **Quantum Compilers** as the official engineering team name.

---

## 3. Consequences
- **Positive:** Distinctive, memorable enterprise brand; clear patriotic alignment with national data sovereignty.
- **Negative:** Requires updating UI strings, logos, and documentation paths.

---

## 4. Security Impact
None.
